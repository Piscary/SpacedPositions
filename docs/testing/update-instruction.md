# Updating Spaced Positions

You are going to update the app to the newest version. **Your puzzles, training progress, and settings are safe** — they live in a separate folder that the update doesn't touch. The app also makes a backup before updating.

## Step 1: Stop the app

If the app is open in your browser, type `:quit` and press **Enter** (the colon opens the command line at the bottom). The page then says "Spaced Positions has stopped", and you can close the tab. The app also stops by itself 10 minutes after you close its last tab, so you could just close it and wait — but `:quit` is faster.

## Step 2: Download the new version

Open this link in your browser:

https://github.com/Piscary/SpacedPositions/releases

On that page, you'll see a **nightly** release at the top (this is the test version). Under **Assets**, download the file for your computer (the names start with `Spaced-Positions-nightly-`):

- **Mac with Apple Silicon** (M1, M2, …): the file that says `macOS-AppleSilicon.dmg`
- **Mac with Intel**: the file that says `macOS-Intel.dmg`
- **Windows**: the file that ends in `.zip`

## Step 3: Replace the old version

**On macOS:**

1. Open the `.dmg` file you just downloaded (double-click it).
2. Drag the new **Spaced Positions** app into your **Applications** folder, replacing the old one. macOS will ask if you're sure — click **Replace**.
3. Eject the disk image (right-click it on the desktop and choose **Eject**).

**On Windows:**

1. Delete the old **Spaced Positions** folder (the one with `Spaced Positions.exe` in it). Your puzzles and training are not in it: they live in their own folder.
2. Unzip the downloaded file (right-click → **Extract All**) and put the new **Spaced Positions** folder where the old one was. Don't copy the new files over the old ones: leftovers of the old version could get mixed in.

## Step 4: Start the app

Double-click **Spaced Positions** again. It will open in your browser as before.

Because the test version isn't signed yet, your computer asks once more whether you trust the new version, just like the first time: on macOS 15 and newer, System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Open Anyway** (older macOS: right-click the app → **Open**); on Windows, **More info** → **Run anyway**.

The first time after an update, it may take a few seconds longer to start — that's normal: the app makes a backup and updates its data folder.

## What to check after updating

1. Does the app start without errors?
2. Are your puzzles and training still there?
3. Is your language setting still correct?

If something is missing, don't try to fix it: tell me. The backup from before the update is in the `backups` folder of the app's data folder (Settings → App shows where), and we can put it back together.
